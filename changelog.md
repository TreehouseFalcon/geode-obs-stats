# v0.1.0

- Added new formatting placeholders for global leaderboard ranks: `{STARS_GLOBAL_RANK}`, `{MOONS_GLOBAL_RANK}`, `{DEMONS_GLOBAL_RANK}`, `{USER_COINS_GLOBAL_RANK}`. These ranks update in the background every 10 minutes by default, and can be configured to update more frequently in the mod's settings.
